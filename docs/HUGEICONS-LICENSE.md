# Hugeicons Free icons in WallyUI

The WallyUI crypto transfer screens use editable vectors generated from
`@hugeicons/core-free-icons` version 4.3.5 (the free Stroke Rounded set):
AddressBook, Blockchain01, Clock01, FingerPrintCheck, ArrowUpRight01,
AlertCircle, CheckmarkCircle01, CancelCircle, and WifiOff01.

Button additions: SecurityCheck, ArrowRight01, ArrowLeft01 (a rotated ArrowRight01), Edit02, Refresh01, Activity01, Save, and Wallet01. These are also from the same free MIT package.

Source: https://github.com/hugeicons/hugeicons
License guidance: https://hugeicons.com/docs/faqs/license-questions

The original draft used the MIT Free package described here. The Android frontend update on 7 October 2026 also imports original assets directly from the user-provided WallyUI Figma file, including layers labeled Hugeicons Pro. Their provenance is recorded in `mobile/assets/wallyui-source.json`.
